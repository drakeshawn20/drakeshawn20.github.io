🌊 What is sbtfix?

sbtfix is a sleek, lightweight application that lives in your macOS menu bar (the top of your screen). It works with your Sony Bluetooth headphones and gives you instant, one-click access to essential controls that normally require you to pull out your phone or fiddle with physical touch buttons.

Think of it as a dedicated remote control for your headphones, always just one click away. It's completely free, open-source, and built with native Swift/SwiftUI for seamless performance.

Step 2: Install on Your Mac

  1. Open the downloaded file. If it's a .zip, double-click to extract it.
  2. Drag the sbtfix app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open sbtfix from your Applications folder. You may see a warning since it's from an unsigned developer — right-click and select Open to override.

Step 3: Connect Your Headphones

  1. Make sure your Sony headphones are already paired with your Mac (via Bluetooth).
  2. Turn on your headphones.
  3. sbtfix will automatically detect them and appear in your menu bar.
💡 Tip: If you don't see sbtfix in your menu bar, make sure your headphones are playing audio or are actively connected to your Mac. If problems persist, restart sbtfix from your Applications folder.

🎮 How to Use sbtfix

Once running, click the headphones icon () in your menu bar. A dropdown panel appears with all controls:

  • Noise Cancelling / Ambient Sound: Click to toggle between the two modes. The current mode is highlighted.
  • Ambient Level: Drag the slider left or right to adjust the amount of ambient sound (only visible in Ambient mode).
  • Speak-to-Chat: A simple on/off toggle.
  • Battery Level: Shown as a percentage and a small battery icon at the top of the panel.

Changes apply instantly — no need to switch anything else on your Mac. Close the panel by clicking anywhere outside of it, or press Escape.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is sbtfix free?

Yes! Completely free and open-source. You can even check out the code on GitHub.

Does it work with any other headphone brands?

No, sbtfix is specifically designed for Sony headphones that use Sony's proprietary wireless protocol. It won't work with other brands.

Will it drain my Mac's battery?

No. sbtfix is extremely lightweight and uses negligible processing power. It's been optimized for efficiency.

What macOS versions are supported?

sbtfix requires macOS 12.0 (Monterey) or later. It works on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs (M1/M2/M3/M4).

How do I update sbtfix?

You can always visit the releases page to see the latest version and download any updates. New versions are released regularly to add features and fix bugs. We recommend checking every month or two.

My headphones aren't detected, what should I do?

  • Ensure your headphones are fully powered on and connected to your Mac (check System Settings > Bluetooth).
  • Restart sbtfix — quit from the menu bar and reopen from Applications.
  • Try re-pairing your headphones with your Mac.
  • If it still doesn't work, check GitHub Issues for known problems or report a new bug.
⚠️ Quick Troubleshooting: If you recently updated your headphone's firmware or your macOS version, try restarting both your Mac and your headphones. This fixes 90% of connection quirks.
